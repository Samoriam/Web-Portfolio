Dear Hiring Manager,

I am excited to apply for the Junior Game Developer position on your team. I am currently earning my Bachelor of Technology in Software Engineering at Western Governors University, and I bring three years of professional experience in AI model quality assurance, prompt testing, and technical documentation. That experience has taught me how to think like a tester, write like a documenter, and collaborate like an engineer, and I am eager to apply all three to building games.

I recently designed and built my own browser-based action game from scratch in JavaScript and HTML5 Canvas. I wrote the game loop, aiming, and collision detection myself, designed difficulty that scales as the player's score climbs, and added a power-up system and automatic gun upgrades to keep each run interesting. I also implemented online two-player matches with PeerJS, so friends can join the same world with a short code, and added touch joysticks so the game plays on phones as well as desktops.

As an AI Trainer at Handshake AI, I evaluate model outputs for quality, consistency, and alignment with project guidelines, and I give structured feedback that improves results. Before that, as a QA Tester and Technical Writer at Outlier AI, I performed detailed testing of large language model outputs, documented edge cases and recurring error patterns, and worked directly with AI engineers and data scientists to refine prompts and training data. I also helped develop evaluation guidelines that kept annotators and QA testers consistent across projects.

My work has shown me that quality software depends on clear, readable code and thorough testing. As software engineer Martin Fowler wrote, “Any fool can write code that a computer can understand. Good programmers write code that humans can understand” (Fowler, 1999, p. 15). I bring that same mindset to my own projects: I write code with the next reader in mind, and I document my work so others can maintain it.

I also bring a strong foundation in data, which helps me balance gameplay and understand players. As a Digital Marketing Specialist at Ace Visions, I used Excel, SQL, Google Analytics, and Power BI to analyze large datasets, and that work helped drive a 20% increase in engagement and a 15% increase in conversion rates. Combined with my programming skills in Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, HTML, and CSS, this experience helps me make careful, data-informed decisions when I build, tune, and debug games.

Thank you for considering my application. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my background in testing, documentation, and game development can support your team. You can view my full résumé and career goals in this portfolio, or reach me directly at samoriamorris1@gmail.com.

Sincerely,
