Career Goals

Where I am today, and where I am headed in software engineering

My Path to Software Engineering

For the past three years, I have worked behind the scenes of artificial intelligence: testing large language model outputs, documenting edge cases, and helping engineers improve model quality. That work showed me how much I enjoy finding problems, explaining them clearly, and helping build better solutions. Now I am turning that experience into a career in software development.

It is an encouraging time to enter the field. The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics projects that overall employment of software developers, quality assurance analysts, and testers will grow 10 percent from 2025 to 2035, much faster than the average for all occupations (U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics [BLS], 2026). My background in both QA and development puts me in a strong position to grow with that demand.

My Goals

Short-Term · 1–2 years

Complete My Degree

  • Earn my Bachelor of Technology in Software Engineering from Western Governors University by November 2027.
  • Build portfolio projects in JavaScript, TypeScript, and Python.
  • Keep growing my QA and technical writing skills in my current AI role.
Mid-Term · 3–5 years

Launch My Development Career

  • Join a team as a junior software developer or QA engineer.
  • Write automated tests and contribute to production code.
  • Learn from senior engineers through code reviews and mentorship.
Long-Term · 5+ years

Build Reliable AI Tools

  • Grow into a software engineer who designs well-tested, well-documented AI-powered tools.
  • Lead quality and testing practices on my team.
  • Mentor others who are moving into tech from non-traditional backgrounds.
